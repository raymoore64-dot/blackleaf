Privacy Policy

Blackleaf by Studio Linicro. Effective 24 November 2026

Summary

Blackleaf does not collect any data. Documents you open are processed only on your device. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we do not operate servers that receive your information. The only network activity is a one-time purchase or restore, which is handled by Apple.

Information processed on your device

When you open a Word document from Files, Mail, the Share sheet or the in-app file picker, Blackleaf copies it into a temporary working folder on your device, with complete file protection, and excludes that folder from device backups. Scanning, review and export all happen on the device. Temporary working copies are deleted when you close the document, when you leave the app’s current document, and the next time the app launches. The original file is only ever read; a new file is written if you export.

The only setting Blackleaf stores is your custom word list, in a protected file in the app’s own storage on this device, excluded from device backups. You can erase that list at any time. Detection mode is not saved between launches.

No accounts or tracking

Blackleaf contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. It does not include a sign-in, and it does not call any Studio Linicro server. Opening, scanning, reviewing and exporting a document all work in Airplane Mode. You can confirm document use with the App Privacy Report in iOS Settings: after normal use, Blackleaf should not show document-related network activity. The only network connection is the optional in-app purchase or restore described below.

In-app purchase

Unlocking export is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles the payment, the receipt and Restore purchase. Studio Linicro does not receive your payment card details and does not run a billing server. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy. Document contents are not sent to Apple or to Studio Linicro as part of a purchase.

Sharing exports

If you choose Share or Save to Files after a successful export, you pick where the new file goes. That sharing is started by you, on your device. Studio Linicro does not receive the file.

Permissions

Blackleaf does not use the camera, photos library, location, contacts, microphone, notifications, Face ID, or tracking permission. It only reads a document you explicitly open.

Children

Blackleaf is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
