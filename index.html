Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Blackleaf has no sign-up, no login and no account.

Where are my documents processed?
Only on your device. When you open a Word file, Blackleaf makes a protected working copy, scans it, and deletes that copy when you close the document or next launch the app. Your original file is never changed. We do not run any servers that receive your documents.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Opening a document, reviewing findings, and exporting a cleaned Word copy or PDF all run on your device, including in Airplane Mode. The only time the app needs a network connection is to buy or restore the one-time unlock, which is handled by Apple.

What files can I open?
Standard Word .docx files, including from Files, Mail or the Share sheet. Blackleaf does not open older .doc files, password-protected documents, macro-enabled files, or Strict OOXML files. It also does not import PDFs. If a file can’t be opened, the app explains why and asks you to re-save it as a standard .docx in Word or Pages.

How do I try it without my own document?
On the home screen, tap Try the sample document. That opens a built-in, fictional tenancy reference so you can review findings and try an export without using a real file.

What can I export?
After you review every finding, you can export a sanitized .docx, or a PDF drawn from that cleaned copy. You can replace text with labels such as [NAME 1] or with fixed-width black bars. An optional Flatten setting turns each PDF page into an image so there is no text layer. Flatten is off by default; if you leave it off, labels in the PDF remain searchable text. Blackleaf re-reads the new file and blocks sharing if a redacted string is still found. Always check the output before you share it.

What does the one-time purchase unlock?
The app is free to open a document, scan it and review every finding. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks export of the sanitized Word copy and the PDF. There is no subscription. Purchases and restores are handled by Apple. A Restore purchase button is on the unlock screen and in Settings.

What doesn’t it do?
Blackleaf does not read text inside images or photos. Embedded objects are listed and removed on export rather than redacted. Charts and some embedded document chunks are listed as not checked. The PDF layout is a simplified rendering of the cleaned Word copy, not a pixel-perfect match for Microsoft Word. Detection can miss things, which is why you review every item before export is allowed.

How can I check that nothing leaves the device?
Turn on Settings > Privacy & Security > App Privacy Report, use Blackleaf normally, then look at App Network Activity. Blackleaf should not appear there. You can also use the app in Airplane Mode; it works the same way.

Where is my custom word list stored?
Only on this device, in a protected file that is excluded from device backups. You can add terms, delete them, or choose Erase word list in Your words.

Is Blackleaf legal, privacy or compliance advice?
No. It is a tool that helps you find and remove personal data, and you review every change. It does not make GDPR, HIPAA, court or legal-compliance claims. Always check the output before sharing.
